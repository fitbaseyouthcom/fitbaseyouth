Workout Plans

Find your perfect plan

Structured programs for every level — from first-timer to experienced lifter.

Beginner

3-Day Full Body

Under 6 months training. Builds foundational strength and movement patterns safely.

  • 3 days per week
  • Full body each session
  • Compound movement focus
  • Linear progression model
  • ~45 min sessions
POPULAR
Intermediate

4-Day Upper/Lower

6–24 months experience. More volume and frequency per muscle group per week.

  • 4 days per week
  • Upper/Lower split
  • Accessory work included
  • Progressive overload
  • ~60–75 min sessions
Advanced

5-Day PPL

2+ years experience. Push/Pull/Legs twice weekly for serious muscle and strength gains.

  • 5–6 days per week
  • Push / Pull / Legs
  • High volume phases
  • Periodization cycles
  • ~75–90 min sessions

Supplements

What actually works

Honest breakdowns based on scientific evidence — not marketing hype.

Well-Supported

Creatine Monohydrate

Most researched supplement in sports science. 3–5g daily increases strength, power, and muscle volume.

Well-Supported

Protein Powder

Convenient way to hit protein targets. Whey absorbs fast post-workout; casein works slower overnight.

Well-Supported

Caffeine

Proven to improve endurance, focus, and strength output. 3–6mg per kg bodyweight pre-training.

Well-Supported

Vitamin D3

Critical for testosterone, bone strength, and immunity. Most people are deficient — 2000–4000 IU daily.

Mixed Evidence

BCAAs

Useful if training fasted. Redundant if daily protein from whole foods is already sufficient.

Overhyped

Fat Burners

Mostly stimulants with minimal real effect. A proper calorie deficit does far more than any pill.

Recovery & Sleep

Where gains are actually made

Muscle is built outside the gym. Recovery is just as important as training.

🌙

Sleep 7–9 hours

Growth hormone peaks during deep sleep. Poor sleep destroys recovery and lowers testosterone.

📅

Rest days

Muscles need 48–72 hours to recover after intense training. At least 1–2 full rest days per week.

🧘

Stretching

Dynamic warm-up before, static stretching after. Reduces soreness and improves long-term mobility.

🚶

Active recovery

Light walks or swimming on rest days boost blood flow and speed up muscle repair without strain.

🧠

Manage stress

Chronic stress raises cortisol which breaks down muscle. Treat stress management like training.
